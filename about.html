About us

Talent was never the problem.

African creativity travels the world. We exist so the people behind it get the structure, skills and spaces to earn from it properly.

Who we are

The Creative Industries Initiative for Africa (CIIFA) is a Lagos company that builds practical things for creative people: a home (the SafeHouse), programmes that teach the business of creativity, and events where talent meets opportunity. We are a business, not a charity, and we measure ourselves by what we deliver.

The numbers so far

12,000+ creatives trained across Nigeria with Google.org. 1,800+ professionals developed through the Music Business Academy. 30+ creative startups supported with the British Council. 50+ talents taken from discovery to career-ready. Programmes delivered with the French Embassy, SACEM and more.

Chocolate City

The foundation we build with

The Creative Talent Foundation (CTF) develops Africa's creative talent through patient, mission-first programmes; CIIFA designs and runs the engine behind them. CBA for Africa, our creative business school, is a CTF programme executed by CIIFA.
