The AMEDI Festival

AMEDI means "welcome" in Ibibio. Once a year, Lagos opens its doors to Africa's creative economy: five days where talent, industry, policy and capital meet in one place.

What it is

The AMEDI Festival is the annual public moment of the CIIFA and CTF ecosystem, and the one national event where everything converges. It is the graduation week of CBA for Africa, the meeting point of the Skill Pool community from first-time explorers to investors, and the week the market comes to meet the talent.

We welcome people to Africa's creative economy: to a career, to the market, to a new class of graduating talent, to the culture across music, film and fashion, and to the table where talent meets capital.

The five days

  • Day 1 · Policy Day : government, regulators and institutions on the structures the industry needs.
  • Day 2 · History Day : the story of African creativity, honoured and documented.
  • Day 3 · Workshop & Conference Day : masterclasses, panels and the working rooms.
  • Day 4 · Trade & Career Fair Day : the Creative Career Fair, deal rooms and the marketplace.
  • Day 5 · Graduation & Finale : the CBA for Africa graduation, capped by a concert, a fashion show and a film showcase in one night.

The Creative Career Fair

The Creative Career Fair now lives inside the festival as Day 4. It is where Africa's creative talent meets the employers, studios and companies that need them: portfolios, hiring conversations and real opportunities in one room. Employers and recruiters who want a stand or a hiring slot should partner with us.

The editions

  • 2026 · CBA for Africa Week : the first edition, the week of 23 November 2026 in Lagos, built around the graduation of the CBA class of 2026.
  • 2027 · The AMEDI Festival : the full five-day festival, 29 November to 4 December 2027, Lagos. One name, every audience, every year after.
Be part of it: sponsorship, delivery partnerships and employer slots are open. Write to info@ciif.africa or visit Partner With Us.
