The foundation

The Creative Talent Foundation

The foundation developing Africa's creative talent through impactful projects and programmes.

What CTF is

The Creative Talent Foundation (CTF) exists to find, develop and advance Africa's creative talent. It backs the programmes that give talent structure: education, skills development and the pathways that turn creative ability into a career.

How CTF and CIIFA work together

The two organisations split the work cleanly. CTF develops talent through impactful projects and programmes. CIIFA runs the business of the creative industries: it designs, executes and scales the platforms, and connects people to information and opportunities.

  • CBA for Africa is a CTF programme, executed by CIIFA: the foundation's flagship investment in creative business education.
  • The Skill Pool Initiative is built together, CTF × CIIFA: the talent ladder the whole ecosystem stands on.
  • The AMEDI Festival is the annual public moment of both organisations, built around the CBA graduation.

Why a foundation

Talent development done properly is patient work. Holding it in a foundation keeps the mission stable while the commercial platforms around it grow: the foundation stays focused on people, and the enterprise keeps the machine running.

Work with CTF: funders and institutions who want to back talent development can start at Partner With Us or write to info@ciif.africa.
