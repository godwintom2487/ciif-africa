A compound where creatives record, rest, meet and host. Three studios, cosy rooms, a meeting room, and a lawn made for good evenings.

The spaces

Everything under one roof at 4B Onikoyi Lane, Parkview Estate, Ikoyi. Prices are confirmed when you book.

Record

Recording studios

Three professional studios, up to 6 people each, with an engineer available for every session.

Stay

Rooms

Standard and master rooms for 2 to 5 guests, with clean linens, AC and WiFi. Master rooms are ensuite.

From ₦45,000 per night · standard ₦45,000–₦50,000 · master ₦55,000–₦65,000
Host

The lawn

Up to 40 guests under string lights, with a bar and a kitchen. Listening sessions, launches, screenings, private dinners.

₦450,000 per 8 hours · Mondays, Saturdays & Sundays · subject to requirements
Meet

Meeting room

A quiet room for up to 8: writing camps, label meetings, pitches and planning days.
