Programmes

Start where you are.

Whether you're curious about the creative industries or already building a company in them, there's a CIIFA programme that meets you there.

The Skill Pool

The free first step. The Skill Pool maps the creative industries into clear paths so you can see where you fit, what to learn, and what each road pays. Start as a complete beginner and climb as far as you want to go.

Ask us about joining →

CBA for Africa

Our flagship: the Creative Business Academy for Africa, a creative business school run with the Creative Talent Foundation. For the founders, managers and operators who want to turn creative work into real companies.

Visit cbaforafrica.org →

Business bootcamps

Short, intense and practical: four-weekend bootcamps in the business of music, fashion and film. No exams, just real deliverables you leave with. Dates for the next cohorts will be announced here and on our socials.

Get notified when dates land →

The AMEDI Festival

AMEDI means "welcome" in Ibibio. Once a year, Lagos opens its doors to Africa's creative economy: five days of ideas, trade, a career fair and one unforgettable closing night. If you only come to one thing we do, come to this.

Partner or exhibit at AMEDI →

The Investors Session

A private room we're designing for the people who fund the creative economy. Invitation only, coming in 2027. If that's you, introduce yourself and we'll keep you close.

Join the invitation list →
